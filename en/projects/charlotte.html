Built solo, for an illustrator · 2026

Charlotte Bastos

Portfolio and online shop for an independent illustrator, fully editable from a CMS.

Illustration, animation and graphic design galleries, an interactive pricing grid, a commission form, an online shop and legal pages, in four languages. All content is edited from an admin studio, without touching the code.

My role. Designed and built end to end: front end, back end, CMS, payments and deployment.

What I did

  • Nuxt 3 and Tailwind: prerendered galleries, server-side rendering for the rest, GSAP animations.
  • Sanity headless CMS: 16 custom schemas with translated fields, so content ships without a developer.
  • Cart and Stripe Checkout payments; a webhook confirms each order and sends an email through Resend.
  • Pricing grid that computes the price from framing, rendering level and extras.
  • French, English, Spanish and German with Nuxt i18n, 20 server API routes.
